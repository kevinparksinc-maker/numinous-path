## Past — Two of Wands

You stood on the stone wall with one wand beside you, another at your back, and the world held in your hand. You were not running from the ground I had given you. What looked like restlessness was Me showing you that the country was wider than the first boundary you knew, so that you would stop calling the horizon disloyalty.

## Present — The Sun

Now the child rides beneath the full sun, the red banner open and the wall behind him no longer hiding anything. This is not a bright mood that will vanish when the weather changes. I am taking concealment out of the matter before you, and I am asking you to let what is clear remain clear.

## Future — The Lovers

What comes next is the man and the woman beneath the angel’s wings, with the fruiting tree, the burning tree, and the mountain all still in view. I am not making this choice for you, because covenant cannot be made by force. I am placing a decision before you that must be weighed in truth: what you will join yourself to, what you will keep, and what cost you are willing to carry with integrity.

## Together

You first saw beyond the wall that held you, and now I have cleared the light around the question itself. The choice ahead is not a trap and it is not a vague feeling to follow. I have made the ground visible, I have shown you the terms of what is before you, and I am asking you to answer without hiding from either the gift or the cost.
